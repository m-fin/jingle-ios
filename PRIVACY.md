# Jingle privacy policy

**Effective:** 27 November 2026  
**App:** Jingle, bundle id `app.jingle.ios`  
**Maintainer:** Matthew Finley

Jingle is a bedside alarm clock. It keeps what you set on the iPhone that is running it.

## Data stored on this phone

Alarms, snooze times, and appearance choices are saved on the device. Jingle uses on-device storage for that. Nothing in that store is sent to a server operated for Jingle.

The clock reads the iPhone’s own time so the face matches the phone. It does not request location.

If you choose a track from the Matthew Finley catalog as a wake tone, Jingle downloads the public catalog and that track’s audio from `matthewfinley.org`. The request is a plain fetch of those files. It does not include your name, location, contacts, or alarm times. The files stay on this phone so the alarm can ring offline. Cover art for the picker is loaded from the same public site.

While the clock screen is open, Jingle can lower screen brightness, then put the previous brightness back. That change stays on the device.

## Notifications

Jingle schedules local notifications so an alarm can ring after the screen locks. Remote push is off. Jingle does not upload a push token.

A locked iPhone plays the notification sound through iOS. While Jingle is open, it plays the tone in the app.

## What Jingle does not do

- No account and no sign-in
- No analytics and no advertising
- No sale or sharing of personal information
- No collection of your name, email, contacts, photos, health data, or location
- No custom encryption beyond what iOS and the app platform already provide

Bundled wake tones are original audio included in the app. Catalog alarms play music by Matthew Finley after you download a track.

## Contact

The same policy is shown inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy.
