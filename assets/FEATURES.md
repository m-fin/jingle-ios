## Hero line
An alarm clock so intuitive it doesn’t even come with an instruction manual.

## Feature blurbs (locked)
1. **One-click time** — Press Sync once; Jingle pulls the time from the air (US WWVB). No dialing hours and minutes by hand.
2. **Sleep in the dark** — The display can go fully blank so nothing glows in your eyes all night.
3. **One alarm. No snooze.** — One wake time. When it rings, you get up — no snooze loop.
4. **Default 6:00 AM** — Ready out of the box for a real morning. Change it only if you want to.
5. **USB-C that does both** — Same plug powers it on the nightstand and charges the battery inside.
6. **Machine mode** — Optional gentle wind-down / pre-wake sound before the real alarm (Sound-owned; not a second alarm).
7. **Looks like night** — Matte Black blends into the dark; Bitcoin Orange is the bold SKU for the stack-sats crowd.
